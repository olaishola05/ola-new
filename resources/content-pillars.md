## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

Your content pillars are the topic territories everything you create lives inside. Every post, video, carousel, or tweet must belong to at least one pillar. When you sit down to create content and do not know what to make, come here first. Each pillar has a definition, a content angle, example topics, and platform fit so you can go from blank page to published in the shortest time possible.

## 08. THE BLANK PAGE RULE

When you do not know what to create, use this decision tree:

1. What pillar have I not posted from in the last 7 days? Start there.
2. What am I actively working on or learning right now? Document it.
3. What question did a student, client, or follower ask me recently? Answer it publicly.
4. What is something I believed 2 years ago that I now know is wrong? Share the shift.
5. What is one thing I know that most people in my space are afraid to say? Say it.

One of these five prompts will always unlock something worth creating.

---

## 09. CONTENT PILLAR CHECKLIST

Before publishing any piece of content, ask:

- [ ] Does this belong to at least one pillar?
- [ ] Does it speak to my audience (beginners, intermediate developers, founders, or solopreneurs)?
- [ ] Is it consistent with my brand voice and boundaries?
- [ ] Does it teach, inspire, or create genuine value?
- [ ] Would I be proud of this in 12 months?

If all five are yes, publish it.
